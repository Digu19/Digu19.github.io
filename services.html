Research support is provided in accordance with ICMJE authorship principles and applicable journal and institutional policies. Substantive contributions must be credited or acknowledged appropriately. I do not offer undisclosed ghostwriting or prepare manuscripts for others to submit as sole-authored work without appropriate attribution. All engagements require scientific independence, confidentiality and conflict-of-interest transparency.

For enquiries, include your project type, discipline, current stage, expected scope and relevant deadlines. Response times depend on availability; no fixed turnaround is promised.

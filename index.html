Research profile

My work bridges clinical nephrology and translational biomarker science. I have experience in urinary and plasma extracellular-vesicle isolation, MACSPlex multiplex bead-based flow-cytometric phenotyping, prospective human cohorts, clinical biomarker studies and multicentre research, with international experience across Italy, Denmark and India.
